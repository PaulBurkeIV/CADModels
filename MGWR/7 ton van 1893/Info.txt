MGWR 7 TON VAN
++++++++++++++

These were built from 1893 onwards, a modernisation of an earlier 1872 design. 
The open centre section remained popular in Ireland long after it became rare
on British lines, perhaps because it was easily adapted for cattle transport-
this being a major aspect of Irish transportation.

They remained in use until after the Second World War (Emergency).
