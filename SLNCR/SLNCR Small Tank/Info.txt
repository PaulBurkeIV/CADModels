SLNCR Leitrim Class
+++++++++++++++++++

Known as the Small Tanks, these five 0-6-4 tanks were built by Beyer Peacock between
1882 and 1899, and withdrawn between 1947 and the final closure of the line in 1957.
None were preserved. This model represents the last locomotive built, Hazlewood, which was 
chosen because the cab was "modernised" at some stage, making it easier to design as
a 3D printing project.
